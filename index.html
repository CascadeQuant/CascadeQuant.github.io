THE TECHNOLOGY

From market data to a structured trade state

CASCADEQUANT is being developed as a technology-assisted framework that organizes market information into a consistent visual workflow for traders and students.

01

Market Structure

Multi-timeframe market structure and price-action conditions are organized before a trade state is presented.

02

Trade State

A structured trade record can contain direction, entry, profit-booking level, top-up, stop loss, target and status.

03

Visualization

The client-facing experience is designed to present the same authorized trade state through a TradingView-based chart interface.

ABOUT CASCADEQUANT

Technology-assisted trading education

CASCADEQUANT™ is an independent trading-education technology project focused on helping users understand structured market analysis and use technology more clearly.

The project is designed around a controlled trade lifecycle, clear visualization, and education on how to interpret the information presented by the technology.
